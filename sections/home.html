What can we do for you?

Our Services

  1. Traditional Funeral Package

    • Complete arrangement including casket, embalming, viewing (lamay), and transport to the memorial park or church.

  2. Cremation Services

    • Modern cremation facilities with options for urn selection, memorial keepsakes, and ash scattering ceremonies.

  3. Embalming & Preparation

    • Professional embalming, cosmetic restoration, and dressing services performed by licensed morticians.

  4. Casket & Urn Selection

    • Wide range of caskets and urns — from budget-friendly to premium hardwood and bronze finishes.

  5. Church & Memorial Park Coordination

    • Assistance in booking partner churches, chapels, and memorial parks/cemeteries, including burial plot arrangements.

  6. Memorial Video & Tribute Services

    • Custom photo/video tribute compilation shown during the wake or memorial service.

  7. Obituary & Documentation Assistance

    • Help with death certificate processing, obituary publication, and other legal documentation.

  8. Floral & Wreath Arrangements

    • Custom floral tributes, condolence wreaths, and sympathy arrangements delivered same-day.
